# Auth email subjects

Paste each template into Supabase > Authentication > Emails, with these subjects. Generated by `scripts/email/build-templates.mjs`.

| Supabase email | File | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| Invite user | invite.html | You're invited to BlinkSpot |
| Confirm signup | confirm-signup.html | Confirm your email for BlinkSpot |
| Magic link | magic-link.html | Your BlinkSpot sign-in link |
| Reset password | reset-password.html | Reset your BlinkSpot password |
| Change email address | email-change.html | Confirm your new email for BlinkSpot |
| Reauthentication | reauthentication.html | Your BlinkSpot code |
